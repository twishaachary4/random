The room

Built for waiting out weather.

Low light, deep chairs, and coffee poured slow enough to justify staying past your first cup.

Petrichor House opened in a converted print shop, and we kept the things that made it feel lived-in: the water-stained ceiling beams, the brass fittings gone soft with age, the single skylight that turns any storm into a drumline. Most nights, someone is reading by the window with a coat still dripping on the hook behind them.

We roast in small batches, cut pastries in the morning before the first tram runs, and keep the lights low enough that six in the evening and eleven at night look much the same from your table. If you've come to get out of the rain, we'd rather you didn't rush back into it.

Rainy Day Blend
